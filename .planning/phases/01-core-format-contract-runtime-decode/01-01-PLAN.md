Key load-bearing facts (verified in 01-RESEARCH.md "Code Examples" against the Blender `namer_core.py` source):
- Octahedral ENCODE is the Blender barycentric projection of the RAW `[0,1]` DirectX normal-map texel: `oct.x = 0.5 + 0.5*v.x/(v.x+v.y+v.z)` (same for y). No corner-fold. R/G always in `[0.5, 1.0]`.
- Octahedral DECODE is the full quadratic inverse `|p|^2 * S^2 - 2S + 2 = 0` (larger root), which recovers the true unit tangent normal AND un-flips the DirectX green channel. Do NOT use a "simple" inverse (it returns the wrong direction — Pitfall 3).
- Roughness quantization is LINEAR: `floor(roughness * 63)`, clamp to [0, 63]. Metallic bit = `metallic > 0.5`; emissive bit = `emissive > 0.1` (both STRICT inequalities — Pitfall 5).
- AO is the B channel with NO transform.
