## Decisions Made

- Chose CPU Burst raycast over GPU depth-projection for the bake (determinism + headless testability, per RESEARCH). The hand-rolled BVH and Möller–Trumbore are the only genuinely hand-rolled pieces, by necessity (Unity.Mathematics ships no geometry queries).
- Empty/uncovered texels use an AO `-1` sentinel in the bake buffer; the pipeline writes them as white (AO=1.0, invalid) so the bilinear upsample and JFA never produce black seam artifacts.
- `RequestBake` is synchronous rather than `EditorApplication.delayCall`-based (see deviation) — still off the debounce and cached.
- Added `Unity.Burst`/`Unity.Mathematics` to the Editor asmdef (the only asmdef change this plan required).
