<objective>
Build the CPU algorithmic core of vertex-color decomposition: a seam-safe, attribute-preserving mesh splitter (VCOL-02) and a Burst-compiled per-triangle barycentric least-squares fitter that quantizes fitted colors to Color32 with a fit-quality alpha (VCOL-01, D-04). This deliberately diverges from the Blender reference's per-loop nearest-texel averaging (D-03) — the fit is a multi-sample 3x3 normal-equations solve per split vertex, running headless and deterministically so downstream plans (04-02 residual, 04-03 wiring) and tests consume concrete, already-verified types.

Purpose: Produce the two CPU mesh-topology stages that turn a source mesh + a linear base readback into per-vertex quantized colors, in the correct order (split FIRST, then fit — a UV-seam-shared vertex must become two distinct vertices before it can hold two different colors, otherwise the fit averages across seams and VCOL-02 is unsatisfiable).

Output: `Editor/Decompose/MeshVertexSplitter.cs`, `Editor/Decompose/VertexColorFitter.cs`, `Tests/Editor/VertexColorDecompTests.cs` (headless EditMode suite).
</objective>
