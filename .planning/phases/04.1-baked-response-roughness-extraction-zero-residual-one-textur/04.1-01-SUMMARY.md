## Decisions Made
- Rec.601 luminance for the Sobel estimator (not the Rec.709 `NAMER_LUMA_*`) to match Blender's `extract_roughness` — flagged in the `.hlsl` doc comment.
- The roughness override is applied at PACK time in `CSSurfacePack` rather than re-dispatching `CSNormalize`, per the plan's explicit instruction (avoids double-applying the AO un-multiply).
- `RoughnessExtractStrength` has no inline default, matching `AoUnmultiplyStrength`; the identity 0 = off preserves the legacy scalar path for direct `Process` callers and existing no-map fixtures.
