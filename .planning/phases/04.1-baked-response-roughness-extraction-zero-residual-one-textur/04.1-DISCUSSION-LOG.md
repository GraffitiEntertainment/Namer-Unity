## Remap formula

| Option | Description | Selected |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Dip-depth: `saturate(scalar − strength·mag/p90)` | Scalar is a true anchor at every strength; strength scales only the edge dip; Sobel texture stays direct-polarity; identical to endpoint-lerp on Neo (scalar = 1) | ✓ |
| Endpoint-lerp: `lerp(scalar, 1 − mag/p90, strength)` | Discards the authored scalar at full adoption (~0.9); glossy-authored sources re-level matte; needs 1−x inversion at both consume sites | |

**User's choice:** Dip-depth (Recommended).
**Notes:** Advisor research (gsd-advisor-researcher) — the two formulas are numerically identical for Neo; dip-depth preserves the anchor invariant for glossy sources where endpoint-lerp breaks it precisely at full adoption. Accepted limitation: edge clamp-at-0 (mirror band) when scalar < strength on mid/glossy sources.
