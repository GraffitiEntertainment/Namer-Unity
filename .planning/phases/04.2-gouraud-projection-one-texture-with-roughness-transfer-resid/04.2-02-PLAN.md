<objective>
Deliver the roughness-transfer half of the phase as pure additive machinery: the
removed-luminance kernel (signed Rec.601 luma of source − projected), the p90
normalization readback (mirroring the established RobustSobelScale pattern), and the
transfer remap kernel that replaces the retired Sobel dip source at the surviving D-08
consume site with saturate(scalar − strength · luma/p90).

Purpose: the removed detail must be visibly re-expressed as gloss variation, not lost
(ROADMAP criterion 2). One signed signal serves both polarities (bright speckle dips
toward gloss AND dark occlusion raises toward matte — locked user decision 2026-09-16;
Neo is 66.9% dark / 33.1% bright removed-luma texels). Chroma grain has no home in a
scalar gloss channel and is an accepted, documented loss (CONTEXT locked).
Output: two new kernels in NAMERRoughness.compute, ExtractTransferRoughness +
RemovedLumaP90 in NamerRoughnessPipeline (nothing deleted — the Sobel path survives as
the alternate dip source per CONTEXT discretion + research recommendation), and a new
test suite proving polarity, headroom, and both identity cases.
</objective>
