for each of the before and after windows should really be based on width/height in
screen space with like 10-20 pixels around the edges of the before/after windows."

The current framing computes ONE rotation-invariant pair-plus-gap size
(`_framedHalfWidth = HalfSeparation + radius + PreviewGap`) across the whole 2:1 rect,
and draws the two meshes at a fixed world offset (±HalfSeparation = 0.7). Because the
separation is a fixed world constant while the orthographic size varies, the pair is
centered as a unit: zooming in pushes the mate object out of its pane, and the initial
view leaves a large gap between small objects. The fix frames EACH pane's object
individually and re-derives the pane-center offset from the orthographic size.

Design decision (documented for the checker): the per-pane fit is computed from the
rotation-invariant bounding sphere (extents.magnitude) at Frame time (neutral rotation).
Because the sphere bounds the silhouette at ANY yaw/pitch, the fit holds under orbit —
no re-fit on orbit is needed, and Frame's existing reset of yaw/pitch to neutral is
kept. The margin constant is MarginPx = 15f (the middle of the user's 10-20 px range),
pinned in tests.

The window (NamerEditorWindow.cs) is NOT touched: DrawPreviewSection already hands the
renderer a single 2:1 previewRect, and the pane split is entirely internal to
NamerPreviewRenderer (left half = before, right half = after). This keeps GAP-2 fully
independent of GAP-3 (which edits NamerEditorWindow.cs) — no file overlap.

Output: reworked NamerPreviewRenderer.cs, flipped framing tests, full EditMode green.
