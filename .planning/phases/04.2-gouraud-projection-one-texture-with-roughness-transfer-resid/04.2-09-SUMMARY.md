# Phase 04.2 Plan 09: Divider-Anchored Tight-AABB Framing + Pane Outline (GAP-4) Summary

**The preview now behaves as two true side-by-side windows: each model hugs the center divider with a pinned 10 px inner margin (LEFT right-aligned, RIGHT left-aligned) under a tight neutral-AABB fit, zoom grows each model outward from the divider (never across it), and a 1 px pane outline marks the window bounds — closing the GAP-4 UAT verdict that per-pane-centered placement wasted the middle, zoom spilled across the divider, and no visible pane bounds existed (UI-03/UI-04).**

## Accomplishments

- `NamerPreviewRenderer` reworked per the GAP-4 UAT verdict (which supersedes BOTH the 04.2-05 pair-plus-gap framing AND the 04.2-07 per-pane-centered placement): `InnerMarginPx = 10f` (the user's verbatim inner margin) and the surviving `MarginPx = 15f` re-scoped to the outer/vertical edges. The rotation-invariant `_framedRadius` is gone, replaced by `_framedHalfWidth` / `_framedHalfHeight` captured from the neutral-rotation `bounds.extents.x/.y` (per-axis fallback 1f).
- `OrthographicSizeForAspect(aspect, rectHeightPx)` now fits the tight neutral AABB with asymmetric horizontal margins: `horizFit = hw * H / max(paneWidthPx - InnerMarginPx - MarginPx, 1)`, `vertFit = hh * H / max(H - 2*MarginPx, 1)`, return `max(horizFit, vertFit) * clamp(zoomScale)`.
- Two new public accessors: `HorizontalHalfExtentWorld()` (the rotated-AABB X half-extent = `|m00|*ex + |m01|*ey + |m02|*ez` of `Matrix4x4.Rotate(Euler(pitch, yaw, 0))`) and `PaneAnchorWorld(aspect, rectHeightPx)` (= `HorizontalHalfExtentWorld() + InnerMarginPx * 2*orthoSize/rectHeightPx`).
- `Render` now draws the before/after meshes at `±PaneAnchorWorld(aspect, rect.height)` (minus the rotated bounds center) instead of `±paneCenterWorld` — the anchor is recomputed per Render, so the inner edge stays exactly `InnerMarginPx` from the divider at any zoom and any orbit, and zoom grows each model outward (never across the divider; outer/top/bottom clipping is accepted window behavior). The shared yaw+pitch quaternion, light rotations, and `_preview.Render(true)` are byte-identical; `Orbit`/`Zoom` untouched.
- `NamerEditorWindow` gained a single private `DrawPreviewPaneOutline(Rect previewRect)` (1 px outer border + 1 px center divider in `Color(0.4, 0.4, 0.4, 1)`) called once, immediately after `GUI.DrawTexture` — a window-side IMGUI overlay on the returned texture, not a baked-in RT pass. `HandlePreviewCameraInput`'s `Orbit(current.delta.x, current.delta.y)` / `Zoom(current.delta.y)` call sites, the debug-channel toolbar, and the debounced recompute are byte-identical in behavior.

## Decisions Made

- The fit is the tight neutral-rotation AABB half-extents, not the bounding sphere. The sphere over-approximates a near-cubic mesh by up to ~1.7x (`r = sqrt(ex^2+ey^2+ez^2)` vs `ex`), which is exactly why the 04.2-07 models rendered too small and left a large empty middle.
- `InnerMarginPx = 10f` — the user's verbatim "10 pixel left/right margins" this round. `MarginPx = 15f` survives, re-scoped to the OUTER/vertical gutter (the surviving round-1 "10-20 px around the edges" on the non-divider sides).
- Anchoring, not fitting, absorbs rotation: the fit is fixed at `Frame` (neutral AABB), and `PaneAnchorWorld` recomputes the rotated horizontal half-extent per Render so orbit never pushes the inner edge across the divider.
- The pane outline is drawn by the window as a 1 px IMGUI overlay on the returned preview texture, not baked into the RT by the renderer. Baking it would require GL immediate-mode lines between `_preview.Render(true)` and `_preview.EndPreview()` (an order-sensitive pass needing an internal shader material) — fragile and hard to verify headlessly. The IMGUI overlay is pixel-exact and the window already owns `previewRect`.
