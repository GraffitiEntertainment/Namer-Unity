<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase replaces the fit-driven sharp-removal search (the 04.1 estimator whose LOOK was rejected in UAT round 5) with a by-construction residual collapse and a roughness-transfer path:

- The cleaned base becomes the **per-triangle Gouraud-representable projection of the albedo**, so the residual quotient (`cleanedBase / vcInterp`) is white up to Color32 quantization — the ladder search, the honest-threshold gamble, and any correction-mask pass retire.
- The removed detail (albedo − projected) has its **luminance transferred into the 6-bit roughness map** via the surviving D-08 dip consume site; dip depth is a pure taste slider.
- A **residual on/off checkbox** overrides the (now near-always-passing) residual gate explicitly.
- The **estimator dropdown consolidates** to one extraction path + decomposition on/off.
- **UV-overlap texels** are the one known exception to "white by construction" and get an honest treatment (research-gated).

Out of scope: stylization (Phase 5), Blender literal fixtures (v2), any change to the packed format contract itself.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Residual collapse
- The cleaned base is the projection of the albedo onto the Gouraud-representable space (per-triangle, reusing the existing 16×16 barycentric fit machinery, writing the fitted surface back) — the base says exactly what the VCs can say, so `base/vcInterp ≡ white` up to Color32 quantization.
- The strength ladder search, the FitOnlyMaxError threshold gamble, and a residual-correction mask pass all RETIRE (locked — round-2 design supersedes round-1's mask idea).

### Roughness transfer
- The removed signal (albedo − projected) is split luminance/chroma; the **luminance component modulates the roughness map** through the surviving D-08 consume site (`saturate(scalar − strength·mag)`); the *source signal* changes from Sobel-of-base to the transferred removed-detail luminance.
- **Chroma grain is an accepted loss** — a scalar gloss channel has no home for it; the loss must be bounded and documented.
- **Polarity by phenomenon**: bright baked speckle → dip toward gloss is physical; dark baked occlusion → matte is a stylization choice — the transfer function needs ONE decided sign treatment for dark response.
- **6-bit roughness saturation headroom**: heavy transfer can clip at both ends; the plan must handle it.

### Dip depth control
- **Dip depth is a pure taste slider** (user decision, round 2) — no precompute, no search; nothing is precomputed against residual whiteness.

### Residual gate
- An explicit **residual on/off checkbox** overrides the near-always-passing gate ("drop it, I accept the quantization dust"); one-texture outcome (no residual EXR) is the default.

### Estimator UX
- The estimator split **largely retires**: one extraction path + decomposition on/off. Whether Sobel-of-base survives as an alternate dip source is a planning call (see Discretion).

### Test posture
- Existing pinned 04.1-07 dip-coupling tests **flip again, intentionally**; the full EditMode suite must end green.

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact projection kernel form (likely reuses the 16×16 fit machinery; how the fitted surface is evaluated/written back into the base texture).
- Whether the Sobel-of-base estimator survives as an alternate dip source, and what the estimator dropdown becomes (renaming is a UX decision).
- The transfer function shape (how removed-luminance magnitude maps to dip depth at the consume site) short of the locked polarity/headroom constraints.
- UV-overlap strategy, informed by the research question: detect-and-report (exclude overlaps from FitOnlyMaxError), last-wins vs. highest-UV-area-triangle-wins, per-island re-rasterization, import-time repair, or an honest "these spots stay" statement.
- Implementation structure across compute kernels / C# pipeline / UI, test organization.
</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

Research-verified mechanics the design depends on (from the PRD, with its file:line evidence):
- Residual quotient = `cleanedBase / vcInterp` in linear space, vc floor 1e-3 (`Compute/NAMERDecomp.compute:130-141`) — the dividend is the CLEANED base, never the source albedo.
- VCs are per-triangle barycentric LSQ on a 16×16 interior grid, unweighted vertex mean, piecewise-linear Gouraud output, Color32-quantized (`Editor/Bake/VertexColorFitter.cs:104,265-270,389-415`) — mid frequencies are structurally unrepresentable, which is why projection (not better smoothing) is the collapse mechanism.
- Overlapping UVs rasterize first-covering-triangle-wins (`Compute/NAMERDecomp.compute:111-113`) — unfixable by projection; likely part of Neo's scattered non-white residual spots.
- The current smoother is a single global texel-space gaussian (radius `clamp(max(w,h)/32, 8, 64)`, σ = radius/3; `NamerRoughnessPipeline.cs:48-51`); `saturate()` in `CSRoughnessSharpRemoval` is mathematically inert.
- Literature pointers flagged ASSUMED/unverified in the PRD: bilateral filtering (Tomasi & Manduchi 1998), Mesh Colors (Yuksel et al. 2010), xatlas-style seam-aware dilation.
</specifics>
