## Coverage-target UX (revisited from fixed 99%)

| Option | Description | Selected |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| A. Coverage slider | Float slider 0.90–1.00, default 0.99, beside the error-threshold slider, persisted in NamerProcessorSettings | ✓ |

**User's choice:** A — coverage slider
**Notes:** Two orthogonal axes ("how wrong is wrong" vs "how much of the image may be wrong") expressed honestly; follows the D-15 slider precedent. User's 512×512 observation implies targets near 99.5% matter, which buckets can't reach.
