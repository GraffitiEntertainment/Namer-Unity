## Goal Achievement

The core outcome is observably present in the codebase. The strict `evalMaxError > errorThreshold` break is gone (zero occurrences), replaced by a percentile gate that breaks on `evalCoverage < target` after `Mathf.Clamp(coverageTarget, 0f, 1f)`. `EvaluateResolution` now returns the covered-normalized within-tolerance fraction (same math as `BuildStats`), and `AchievedCoverage` is populated on every stats path. Both runtime-verified tests (`IgnoresErrorTail` → rung 128 at target 0.99 despite `MaxError > threshold`; `TargetStrictnessMovesTheRung` → 256 fallback at target 1.0) discriminate the new gate from the old one.
