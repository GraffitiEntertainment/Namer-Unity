**Source:** `NamerStylePostFit.cs:177-189` (class doc), :206 (`kExposureBisectionIterations = 48`), :211 (`kWhiteBalanceGridSteps`); `NamerStyleExtractor.cs:56-68` (class doc).

### NaN-then-clamp sanitization
**Source:** `NamerPostLook.ClampToCaps` :225-313 (NaN falls back to identity BEFORE `Mathf.Clamp` — clamp propagates NaN); `SanitizeCurve` :335-357.
**Apply to:** every new serialized field on `NamerStyleAsset` (grade params, confidence stats, palette entries), consumed at Decompose time and Volume-write time (T-05.2-01-01 precedent).
